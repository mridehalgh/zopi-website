# Marketing research and packaging handoff

Reviewed 2026-11-21. Sources: repository product brief, public website source and official competitor pages. This is a dated research snapshot, not verified customer research or evidence that the competitors deliver their advertised outcomes. No prospects or vendors were contacted.

## Positioning decision

Lead with UK and EMEA suppliers needing retailer EDI, with a second route for integration teams. The strongest initial hypothesis is an approachable managed service with clear costs and flexible commitment. Enterprise breadth, APIs and agent tools support technical confidence after the supplier understands the offer.

Preserve the direct, approachable voice in `PRODUCT.md`. Replace abstract promises with the documents, responsibilities and next steps a supplier recognises. Retailer logos indicate connection coverage, not customer endorsement. Treat coverage and capabilities asserted in the product brief as claims requiring operational evidence before stronger public wording.

## Current price and proposed package research

Current repository rate: £49.50/month plus £0.45 per business document, excluding VAT. Standard trial: 30 days. Switching offer: 60 days with eligibility confirmation. These are existing website terms, not newly validated billing policy.

| Documents/month | Existing standard monthly total, excluding VAT |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | £49.50 |
| 100 | £94.50 |
| 500 | £274.50 |
| 1,000 | £499.50 |
| 2,000 | £949.50 |
| 5,000 | £2,299.50 |

At 2,000 documents FTP EDI publishes £249 versus the current EDI Spark formula's £949.50. Counting policies, partner allowances, features and service scope differ. This is a reason to research volume packaging, not a defensible blanket savings claim.

Retain existing prices until the commercial owner approves a replacement. Starter/Growth/Enterprise below is a **research proposal**, not an offer. Keep the free developer sandbox separate from commercial supplier packages.

| Proposed package | Intended buyer | Package hypothesis to validate | Price approval status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | Supplier beginning retailer EDI at low volume | Managed core document exchange, clear allowance and support scope | Monthly rate, allowance and overage intentionally unapproved |
| Growth | Growing supplier with larger volume/integration needs | Higher included volume and service scope supported by delivery economics | Monthly rate, allowance and overage intentionally unapproved |
| Enterprise | Complex networks, procurement or service requirements | Scoped agreement, capacity and service commitments | Custom quotation; no invented minimum or discount |

Do not invent volume discounts, unlimited support, migration guarantees, included ERP work or SLAs. Approval requires the worksheet and interview evidence below. Any launch price change must state the effective date, which customers it applies to and how existing agreements are handled.

### Unit-economic worksheet

Complete one row per package and low/typical/high-volume scenario. Use actual source records, not inferred averages.

| Input | Record |
|---|---|
| Monthly base, included documents, overage rate, other fees | Proposed values and commercial owner |
| Documents exchanged; billable subset; technical acknowledgements/retries | Measured distribution and agreed counting policy |
| Platform, transport and storage costs | Monthly allocated amount and per-document variable cost |
| Onboarding/mapping/testing effort | Hours × loaded rate; amortisation period stated separately |
| Ongoing monitoring/support | Hours × loaded rate by scenario, including incident work |
| Third-party connector/licensing costs | Recurring and one-off costs with source |
| Revenue | Base + max(0, billable documents − allowance) × overage + approved fees |
| Contribution and gross margin | Revenue − agreed direct delivery costs; margin = contribution / revenue |
| Fully loaded acquisition cost | Paid spend + marketing/sales labour + tools + contractors / acquired paying accounts over matching closed windows |
| Payback, retention, expansion | Cohort data and observation window; unknown until measured |

Define the direct-cost accounting basis once. Test peak months, high support, many low-volume partners, early cancellation and migration effort. Do not equate high document volume with high support effort without evidence.

### Eight supplier interviews

Recruit three suppliers starting retailer EDI, three using an incumbent, and two with higher-volume/integrated operations. Record consent and anonymise notes. This is directional qualitative research, not statistically representative demand estimation.

1. Ask about the last retailer EDI requirement: trigger, deadline, document types and internal owner.
2. Reconstruct the actual onboarding process, delays and work performed by supplier/provider/retailer.
3. Ask what they currently pay, which costs surprised them and how usage changes seasonally; accept “unknown.”
4. Identify decision maker, procurement/security blockers and the operational consequences of failure.
5. Show the revised page; ask them to explain the offer and calculate a bill before explaining it yourself.
6. Compare fixed, allowance/overage and base-plus-usage descriptions in rotated order; ask why each fits or fails.
7. Present candidate prices approved for research; ask too cheap, good value, expensive-but-consider and too expensive. Treat responses as perceptions, not forecast conversions.
8. Ask what proof and migration support would make them take the next step. Capture their exact language and strongest objection.

Output: anonymised notes, segment patterns, contrary findings, copy comprehension failures and a proposed package decision with owner/date. No interview, quote request or outreach has been performed as part of this implementation.
